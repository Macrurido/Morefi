# Records of the bullseye puffer catch landed.

This data set provides records of the bullseye puffer catch landed on
the Pacific coast of Mexico in 2023. It includes information on Biomass
(live weight) for each commercial landed presentation : total weight
(WT), body trunk weight (WB), and fillet weight (Wfi). The data also
indicates whether the catch was Fresh or Frozen-thawed. Values are
provided in Mexican pesos per landed category and per kilogram of landed
weight (SIPESCA, 2024).

## Usage

``` r
Botete_land
```

## Format

\## \`Botete_land\` A data frame with 5 obs. of 5 variables:

- Category:

  Landed presentation.

- Landed:

  Landed weigth by category (g).

- Biomass:

  Converted landed category weigth to total weight (g).

- Pesos:

  Value in Mexican pesos per landed category.

- Pesos_kg:

  Mean value per kilogram of landed weight.

## Source

\<https://github.com/Macrurido/Morefi.git\>

## Details

Category includes five commercial landed presentations: whole fresh
(WT_Fresh); body trunk fresh (WB_Fresh), body trunk Fresh-Thawed
(WB_Fresh_Thawed); fillet fresh (Wfi_Fresh), and the sum of total
(Total).
